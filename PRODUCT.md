# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Solo travellers, couples, groups, first-time international travellers and busy professionals, discovering JM
Travels primarily through Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp and referrals rather than search. They are deciding whether
to trust a brand-new travel consultancy with planning a real trip. Skews younger and mobile-first: scrolling on a
phone between other things, not sitting down at a desk with intent to compare five agencies.

## Product Purpose

Convert social and referral discovery into a WhatsApp conversation or consultation enquiry, by making a
week-old business feel like an obviously capable one. The website is the credibility layer for a founder-led
travel planning business (organised trips, bespoke trips, visa assistance, consultations) that is honest about
being new while proving it already knows what it's doing.

## Brand Personality

Warm, confident, editorial-premium, and alive: a well-travelled friend who happens to be excellent at logistics,
not a corporate agency. The site should read as current and human, not stiff or templated. Jemima ("the Lead
Traveller," not "the Founder") is the face and voice of the brand — the copy should sound like she's actually
talking, not like a company statement.

## Anti-references

- Generic travel-agency templates and crowded package-card layouts dominated by prices and badges.
- Overuse of bright tropical colour, heavy gradients, glassmorphism, or gimmicky animation.
- Overly corporate, blue-only, cold interfaces; anything that reads as "visa agency" or "mass-market tour operator."
- Cheap-looking stock photography; excessive gold that reads as jewellery/financial-services rather than travel.
- Visible AI-generated-content tells: decorative em dashes, the "—— LABEL" eyebrow-dash motif, gradient text,
  hero-metric-card templates, identical icon+heading+text card grids, side-stripe accent borders.

## Design Principles

1. Honest credibility over manufactured authority: never fabricate testimonials or a track record the business
   doesn't have yet; the newness itself is part of the story.
2. Restraint as the premium signal, not the enemy of energy: alive and current doesn't mean loud or gimmicky.
3. Editorial photography-led storytelling over stock-photo clutter or plain text blocks.
4. WhatsApp-first, mobile-first conversion: assume the visitor is on a phone, between other things.
5. Every interactive surface should feel touched, not static: motion and micro-interaction carry personality
   the copy alone can't.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` on all scroll/hover motion. Visible keyboard focus states,
comfortable touch targets, never colour-alone for state or error.
