Five days is enough time to see Dubai properly without feeling like you're rushing between checklist items. Here's a structure that balances the major sights with rest.

## Day 1 — Arrival and Marina

Settle in, then head to Dubai Marina for the evening. A dhow cruise is a low-effort, high-reward way to spend your first night.

## Day 2 — Downtown

Burj Khalifa in the morning before the heat and crowds build up, followed by Dubai Mall and the Dubai Fountain show in the evening.

## Day 3 — Desert safari

An afternoon and evening desert safari — dune driving, sandboarding if you're up for it, and dinner under the stars. Plan for a slower morning beforehand.

## Day 4 — Old Dubai

Abra ride across Dubai Creek, the gold and spice souks, and a slower pace than the previous two days.

## Day 5 — Free day and departure

Keep this day open. Beach time, shopping, or an optional extra like a yacht trip — whatever the trip has been missing so far.

## The one mistake to avoid

Packing every day back-to-back with a fixed schedule. Dubai's heat (especially outside the winter months) makes a rigid itinerary harder to enjoy than it looks on paper. Build in at least one genuinely free stretch.
