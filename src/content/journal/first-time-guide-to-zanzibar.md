Zanzibar rewards a bit of planning. It's a small island with two very different experiences on offer — Stone Town's history and markets, and the beach towns that stretch along the north and east coasts. Most first-time mistakes come from trying to do both without enough time, or picking accommodation in the wrong spot for what you actually want from the trip.

## When to go

June to October is the most dependable window — dry, warm, and clear of the long rains that can disrupt beach days between March and May. December to February is also good, though it's the busiest and most expensive stretch.

## Stone Town or the beach — or both?

If you have four nights or fewer, we'd usually recommend picking one and doing it properly rather than splitting your stay across two bases and losing time to transfers. With five nights or more, a day or two in Stone Town followed by three-plus nights on the coast gives you both without feeling rushed.

## Getting around

Transfers from the airport to the north coast take around 90 minutes to 2 hours depending on traffic and your exact destination. Arrange this in advance rather than negotiating on arrival.

## A realistic first-timer's structure

- **Day 1:** Arrive, settle in, no plans.
- **Day 2:** Stone Town guided walk, market, lunch, back to the coast by evening.
- **Day 3–4:** Beach, snorkelling, one sunset dhow cruise.
- **Day 5:** Departure.
