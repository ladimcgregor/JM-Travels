I've spent years travelling widely at this point, planning most of those trips myself, plus a good number for
family and friends along the way. Somewhere in that process, planning stopped feeling like a chore and started
feeling like the part I actually enjoyed: figuring out how the pieces of a trip fit together so the person
travelling doesn't have to think about it.

JM Travels started from a simple observation: most people don't struggle to *want* to travel. They struggle to
plan it properly, sequencing a trip, knowing what's worth the time and what isn't, sorting out visas and
logistics, and making decisions with confidence instead of guesswork.

That's the gap I built JM Travels to close. Not by pretending to be a company with decades of history, but by
being honest about where we're starting from (real, practical travel experience) and building on it properly, one
trip at a time. Every trip we plan for a customer teaches us something we'll use on the next one: a better
supplier, a smarter itinerary sequence, a detail we hadn't thought to ask about before.

If you're planning a trip and want someone who actually thinks about this the way you'd want a friend with good
travel judgement to, that's what I'm here for.

Jemima, Lead Traveller at JM Travels
