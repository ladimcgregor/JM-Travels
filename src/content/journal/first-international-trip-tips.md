Most first-trip mistakes aren't dramatic. They're small, avoidable things that add friction or cost. Here are ten worth knowing in advance.

1. **Check your passport's expiry date now.** Many countries require at least six months of validity beyond your travel dates.
2. **Confirm your visa requirement early**, not the week before you travel. Some processes take weeks.
3. **Photocopy or photograph your documents**: passport, visa, insurance, itinerary, and store them separately from the originals.
4. **Tell your bank you're travelling** to avoid your card being blocked for "unusual activity."
5. **Buy travel insurance before you need it**, not after something goes wrong.
6. **Don't overpack your itinerary.** One or two solid activities a day beats five rushed ones.
7. **Arrive at the airport earlier than feels necessary** for your first international flight; you'll move slower through an unfamiliar process.
8. **Keep a digital and physical copy of your accommodation confirmations.**
9. **Research local etiquette and dress norms**, particularly for religious or conservative sites.
10. **Build in one buffer day** somewhere in the trip, for delays, tiredness, or simply changing your mind about the plan.

None of this is complicated, but it's easy to skip when you're excited about the destination and not thinking about the logistics. That's usually where a [consultation](/consultations) earns its keep: an outside check on the plan before you commit to it.
